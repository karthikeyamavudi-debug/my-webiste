Karthikeya is a name associated with courage, determination, knowledge, and strength. Karthikeya is known as the son of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati in Hindu mythology and is also called Murugan, Skanda, Subrahmanya, and Shanmukha. He is traditionally described as a powerful warrior who represents bravery and the victory of good over evil. The name Karthikeya is connected with the six Krittika stars, which is why he is often depicted with six faces, symbolizing different forms of wisdom and awareness. He is commonly shown carrying the divine spear called the Vel and riding a peacock, which represents confidence and the ability to overcome pride and negativity. Karthikeya is especially worshipped in South India and is an important deity in Tamil culture, where he is affectionately known as Murugan. Many temples dedicated to him are found across India and in other parts of the world. Beyond mythology and religious traditions, the name Karthikeya can also represent qualities such as discipline, confidence, leadership, and the courage to face challenges. A person named Karthikeya can take inspiration from these values by working hard, learning from mistakes, helping others, and staying determined even when situations become difficult.
